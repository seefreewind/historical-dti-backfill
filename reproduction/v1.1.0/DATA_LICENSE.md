# Data license and attribution

ChEMBL data are from https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/. The releases used are ChEMBL 24.1, 27, 29, 31, 33, 35, 36 and 37, maintained by EMBL-EBI. Release-specific source URLs, archive filenames and checksums are in manifests/RELEASE_SOURCES.tsv and manifests/RAW_INPUTS.tsv.

ChEMBL-derived records and adaptations in expected/ and source_data/ retain the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/. Consult the linked legal terms. The project's adaptations include qualifying graph filters, release-membership and first-appearance labels, structural/protein mappings and record-level audit classifications. These adaptations and dates are described in the research and audit records. The license on project code is separately MIT; it does not replace the data license.

Original SQLite databases and their raw archives are external prerequisites and are excluded by project policy. Additional third-party rights or notices, when applicable, remain separate. Author-controlled manuscript and supplementary content require final author approval before public release. No new public release has been authorized.
