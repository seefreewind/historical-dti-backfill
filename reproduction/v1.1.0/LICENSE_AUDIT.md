Project code: MIT, with the existing LICENSE retained. ChEMBL-derived activity, graph, structural, mapping and source tables: CC BY-SA 3.0 with source/release attribution. These data are not relicensed as MIT. ChEMBL official FAQ permits redistribution/adaptation subject to attribution and share-alike; raw SQLite exclusion is a project restriction, not an asserted blanket legal ban. Source https://chembl.gitbook.io/chembl-interface-documentation/frequently-asked-questions/general-questions and https://chembl.github.io/chembl-licensing/ checked 19 November 2026.

ChEMBL compound property calculations can have commercial software obligations. This workflow uses published structures, activities and mapping evidence, not isolated commercial property outputs to replicate proprietary calculations. Do not add property files without review. Protein sequences/accessions originate in ChEMBL exports; upstream sequence-source obligations require author/license review before public redistribution of full sequence content. The current selected component tables carry accessions and sequence MD5, not full sequence strings.

Dependencies are installed externally, not redistributed as wheels or binaries in the public candidate. Version-specific license metadata and packaged notices must be retained in environment/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.csv and third_party_notices/. Ambiguous data or artwork rights remain LICENSE_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Author-created figure/table artwork is pending author ownership confirmation. No blanket legal clearance is issued by an automated scan.
