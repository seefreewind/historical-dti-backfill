Software is licensed under MIT. ChEMBL-derived data, including tables, molecular
representations, membership/label products and packaged model-score products,
are shared under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Preserve attribution and this
notice when redistributing or adapting these data. The software licence does
not replace the data licence.

Source: ChEMBL, EMBL-EBI, https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/ . Releases 24.1, 27, 29,
31, 33, 35, 36 and 37 are identified in MANIFESTS/CHEMBL_INPUT_MANIFEST.tsv.
Zdrazil et al., The ChEMBL Database in 2023, Nucleic Acids Research 52,
D1180–D1192 (2024), https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad1004 .
Derived transformations include edge filtering and normalization, recovered
release-membership labels, scores, tie-aware summaries, scaffold and chemical
relationship classification. Full original ChEMBL SQLite dumps are excluded;
obtain the specified releases from EMBL-EBI. No patient-level data are included.
